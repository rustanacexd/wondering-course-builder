## What the eval caught

- Both French lessons say every verb of movement takes être (marcher and courir take avoir). The error is in the eval's own passage, which wrote "verbs of movement such as aller, venir...". The generator widened it into a rule. This is guideline 6 (say when a claim applies) failing in the source, not in the brief preamble. The passage and plan are now fixed (only a fixed list of verbs takes être; marcher and courir take avoir). The lessons above were generated before the fix.

## Limits

- Small sample: 10 pairs, 2 reps. Enough to show the direction, not the exact size.
- The judge was not calibrated against the owner's own picks (the owner was away).
- One judge model. Judge notes are in `out/quality.json` to check by hand.
